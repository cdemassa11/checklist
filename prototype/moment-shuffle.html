Things to try
  • Tap Shuffle.
  • Press and hold Shuffle to wind it up, then let go for a harder spin.
  • Drag or fling a blob. It drifts back home.
  • Move your cursor near the blobs. They shy away.
  • Tap a blob to pick that moment yourself.
  • Hit Skip → mid-shuffle to jump to the result.
